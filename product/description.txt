Global soil hydraulic properties from FutureWater HiHydroSoil v2.0 in an Icechunk Zarr v3 store. Two native EPSG:4326 grids at nominal 250 m resolution: 13 continuous properties and soil texture class on six depth intervals to 200 cm, plus 2-D hydrologic soil group. Includes water retention, saturated hydraulic conductivity, Mualem–van Genuchten parameters, and organic matter.

Continuous values are float32 in physical units with NaN for no data; no scaling is required. Texture and hydrologic classes use int16 and uint8, with code mappings in array attributes. Units, CRS, and source provenance are embedded in metadata. Zstd-compressed 64 × 64 spatial chunks in 1024 × 1024 shards support byte-range reads of point profiles and small areas. Mean/mode overviews (2×–256×) support map display. CC-BY 4.0.
